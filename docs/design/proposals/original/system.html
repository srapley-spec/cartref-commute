What the live states borrow

From Commutative: the dark, status-first card; platform as a first-class number; "You're on the…" as a confirmation chip; one instruction in the accent block.

From Flighty: progress against the plan with a delay delta against the timetable; last reported / next stop; share the ETA; Live Activity & Dynamic Island as native extension points.

Better than both: the delta is from Service Details reports, not an estimate, and the onward leg (tube + walk) is already in the number.
