# Review history and resolution status

The findings below are the independent agent’s initial static review, not a list of unresolved production bugs. The current prototype corrects selected-service propagation, homeward sample contradictions, missing Tube/board context, location gating, departure prominence, fallback metadata and navigation continuity. Later browser checks exercised the fixes; see [verification and limits](../design-system.md#independent-review-and-revisions). Remaining coverage gaps include real-device/VoiceOver/contrast validation and the non-simulated failure states listed there. Automatic view refinement was browser-checked after this review.

# Independent prototype review

Static source inspection, 29 October 2026. Reviewed the prototype, design inventory, relevant capability stories and production rendering. No browser rendering, iPhone/VoiceOver, contrast measurement, or live data testing was performed. Findings concern the review artifact and implementation risks, not production regressions already made.

## Fix before presenting the prototype as internally consistent

1. **P1 — Selected Tube identity is lost.** `early-confirm` stores only `Metropolitan`; `tubeView()` treats every Metropolitan as the 07:54. Selecting 07:48 therefore shows office **08:09**, while the toast says **08:03**. Store service departure plus line and derive all subsequent views from it. After choosing 07:54, “Other departures” also repeats the selected train and removes the former Circle option, preventing a direct switch back.
2. **P1 — Train selection does not propagate.** Choosing 17:48 on the home train sheet still renders “18:04 Great Northern”, 18:37 rail arrival and 18:55 home. Selecting the slow 07:00 correctly changes the rail ETA to 07:57, but “Preview the connection” resets arrival to 07:45 and offers already-departed 07:48/07:51 services. Keep one selected-service record across screens.
3. **P1 — Shared homeward examples contradict each other.** The 18:06 Farringdon Thameslink reaches home 19:12 in `trains()` but 18:52 in `tubeHome()`. The alternative 18:24 Great Northern is cancelled in planning but recommended in Tube-home. Derive both from the same service data, or clearly label separate disruption scenarios.
4. **P1 — Location-unavailable view promises not to assume home, then does.** It immediately shows “Cartref → office”, a home-based leave time and recommendation before an origin is chosen. Source production waits for a known direction/origin. Show unresolved state or explicitly identify a user-configured assumed origin.
5. **P2 — Simulated time and certainty undermine the data labels.** All home states display 17:40, including an 18:20 on-train timeline and 18:55 arrival; morning train clock 07:22 pairs “Stevenage 07:20” with “40 sec ago”. Journey’s stale timeline still says “Expected · National Rail” and final copy says rail arrival “is live”. Give each scenario one clock/source timestamp and label stale values throughout.

## Preservation gaps to resolve in the design contract / examples

6. **P1 — Existing homeward Tube choice and full board disappear.** Production `renderHero()` includes `tubesFromAldgate(now)` near the office; the prototype only has a rail-oriented home plan. Production `onTubeHome` retains `renderList` (“Trains home”); the prototype Tube-home screen has only two options. Add the next westbound Tubes with their leave/home consequences and retain the full homeward rail board. These are current functions, not deferred leisure features.
7. **P2 — Walking/station context retains obsolete origin legs.** In both directions `steps()` still starts at home/office even at the station. Walking total duration remains computed from the old home leave time; per-train on-track/behind and departure countdowns are absent. Journey keeps final arrival as its largest time at the station despite the departure-first story. Use situation-derived steps, remaining duration and departure prominence.
8. **P2 — Fallback loses flags and detail access.** The fallback block bypasses `optionFlags()` and has no journey-steps disclosure or door-to-door duration; the full board also has no expandable steps. The documented preservation promise cannot be demonstrated for standing, tight, deadline-late or uncertain alternatives. Keep every option’s flags and route details reachable, including fallback.
9. **P2 — Line status and failure coverage are incomplete.** `lineStatus()` always names Great Northern even on a Thameslink recommendation, omits Hammersmith & City fallback, and labels Tube statuses “Good service” during a stale-feed scenario. The sample selector has no loading, partial-route failure, Tube-feed failure, standing-room or unknown-home ETA state. Add representative states before accepting the system; unknown-home is currently explanatory sheet copy while the hero continues showing a definite ETA.
10. **P2 — Plan/Live can lose active journey context.** “Live journey” always chooses `train`, so returning from Plan while on Tube/final walk returns to the rail screen. Separate active journey state from navigation and retain service, leg and deadline. Switching planning modes also overwrites entered time, despite the persistence contract.

## Accessibility and verification

- `cc-switch button` overrides minimum height to 40px, below the design’s 44px touch target. Much important supporting text is 11–12px, below the system’s proposed 13–14px supporting range. Validate at 320px and enlarged text rather than accepting the phone frame alone.
- Each render replaces both phone trees. The appearance/date/time change handler does not restore input focus; closing a sheet moves focus to the Plan control rather than its opener. Preserve the active control and return focus to the opening action. Sheets are inline regions, so do not require a modal focus trap unless converted to overlays.
- Good foundations: clear illustrative-data label, textual flags, inline platforms, visible direction/pace entry points, retained Settings/Leisure affordances, native details and focus-visible styling. These do not substitute for rendered overflow, contrast and VoiceOver testing.
