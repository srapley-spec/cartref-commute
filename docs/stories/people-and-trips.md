# People and trips
Status: Planned (plan step 3) · Since: 28 Oct 2026

## Story
As a family using one app, I want each trip to know who's travelling, their pace and needs, and any stops and
deadlines, so that the plan fits the slowest of us and the nursery pick-up.

## Where it came from
Requirements set out on 28 Oct, with Laura's commute and family days out as the drivers.

## Acceptance criteria (requirements)
- **People** have a walking pace (Laura: 20% slower than Sam's fast pace, configurable) and needs (buggy →
  step-free).
- **A trip says who's travelling**: the slowest pace and everyone's needs combined.
- **Stops**: e.g. nursery drop-off at St Francis nursery, Letchworth; location and duration configurable, default
  20 min. Available to Sam's profile too.
- **Deadlines**: evening "must pick up by" a set time, which makes the evening an Arrive by.
- Everything personal is configurable in Settings.

## Tests
- None yet (planned). The engine already takes "who's travelling" in its question shape; the made-up corridor
  tests prove places aren't hard-coded.
