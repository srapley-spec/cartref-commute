# Family day out
Status: Planned (plan step 4) · Since: 28 Oct 2026

## Story
As a family with a buggy on a day out, I want step-free routes to anywhere and a "when do we have to leave" from
wherever we are, so that getting home is easy.

## Acceptance criteria (requirements)
- Step-free routes first (buggy); others shown with stairs flagged (never hidden).
- Any destination (e.g. the Science Museum).
- From anywhere (e.g. a pub) home: nearest stop, bus or tube to the main line, then trains, with "leave now" or
  "you have N min" ("finish your pint: leave in 15 min").
- Station facility notices (lifts, escalators), grey for the commute, become relevant here.
- Lift status: TfL lift outages can be checked for TfL stations; for National Rail stations only step-free access
  is known, not outages.
