## 24 Nov 2026 — Web app, iPhone app · #89

**Changes**
- A train that gets you there 10 minutes or more sooner than the one you're following, and that you can still catch, now appears at the top of the card: when you'd arrive, how much sooner, whether it's comfortable or tight to catch, and Switch / Keep buttons. The green "On track" line steps aside while it shows. Under 10 minutes sooner, it stays the quieter "Another option" below the card.
